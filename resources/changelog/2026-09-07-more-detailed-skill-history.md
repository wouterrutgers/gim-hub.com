# More detailed skill history

- Recent skill history now records progress in five minute intervals, with hourly and monthly summaries for older history.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom and drag to pan through your skill history. The level of detail adjusts automatically to the visible period.
- Jump to the past day, week, month, year, or all recorded history using the new period buttons. Reset zoom returns to your last selected period.
- Experience gained and experience per hour now reflect the visible period.
