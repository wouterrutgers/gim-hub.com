# Clearer skill history

- Move through your skill history with a compact toolbar, clearer period buttons, and previous and next controls.
- See each player's progress at a glance and expand their row for the skill breakdown.
- Removed zooming and dragging because the chart kept jumping and reloading, which made it unintuitive to use. If you'd really like zooming back, let me know and I'll take another look.
- Dropdowns now use the same spacing and borders across the app, with more room around the arrow.
