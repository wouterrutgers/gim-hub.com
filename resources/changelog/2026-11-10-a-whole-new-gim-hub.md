# A whole new GIM Hub

- **The new design is here!** GIM Hub just got a full makeover, with a fresh layout, cleaner player panels, and a redesigned mobile experience. Your group's gear, gains, and big moments have a new home.
- Pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **Browntown**. If you're feeling nostalgic for the old look, try **Classic light** or **Classic dark**. You can also adjust the background opacity in **Settings**.
- Added collapsible navigation and a compact mobile menu.
- Browse your group bank in **Compact** or **Detailed** view.
- The **Activity** page now compares teammates' progress side by side, with daily summaries of XP, levels, boss kills, quests, diaries, and drops, plus older weekly summaries.
- Select the same player on each device to sync your theme, display settings, and pinned items across browsers and devices. You can change players from the menu or **Settings**.
- Added a **What's new** popup for new changelog entries. Updates you've already read are remembered for your player across devices.
- Updated the demo with more realistic player stats, equipment, movement, and activity history for every teammate.
- Refreshed game data and improved automatic updates for items, quests, and the world map.
- Updated the self hosting guide to reflect all the latest and most modern changes.
