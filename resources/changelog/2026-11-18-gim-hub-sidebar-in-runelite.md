# GIM Hub sidebar in RuneLite

- Open **GIM Hub** from the RuneLite sidebar to check in on your group while you play.
- In **Members**, see who's online and check your teammates' inventories, equipment, skills, quests, and achievement diaries.
- In **Activity**, follow your group's XP gains, levels, boss kills, quests, diaries, and drops. New activity is highlighted, and **New only** shows what changed since you last opened the tab.
- In **Items**, search across the group's items and filter by teammate or storage location. Your pinned items sync between RuneLite and the website.
- Turn on **Hide sidebar** in the GIM Hub plugin settings if you want to hide the panel.
