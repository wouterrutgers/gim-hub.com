# Wanted list and recent progress

- Open **Wanted** on the **Items** page to set shared item goals, including items your group doesn't own yet.
- See how many your group already owns across teammates and shared storage, and how many are still needed. Goals move to **Completed** when the group reaches the target.
- For items with recipes, see which ingredients you already have and how many more you need. Pick a method when there are several, and expand ingredients to see how to make them.
- The **Group bank** now shows GE and high alch totals at the top, with search and filters grouped together.
- The **Activity** timeline groups active XP gains and boss kills under **Recent progress**.
