# Collection log links and RuneLite improvements

- Click a drop in **Activity**, or a player's drop count in **Compare**, to open that player's collection log with the item highlighted. Drops without a collection log slot link directly to the OSRS Wiki.
- Chambers of Xeric unlocks are now marked as new collection items in **Activity** as soon as the game announces them.
- In the RuneLite sidebar, **Items** lists each storage location on its own line and keeps your scroll position when quantities update.
- The RuneLite **Stats** panel uses regular skill icons, smaller level numbers, and progress bars that more closely match the website.
